\title{When Should an LLM Issue Triager Stay Silent? Calibration, Drift, Rewording and Evidence in Issue Triage}

\author{Sina Gogani Khiabani}
\affiliation{\institution{University of Illinois Chicago}\city{Chicago}\state{IL}\country{USA}}
\email{sgoga3@uic.edu}
\author{Verya Monjezi}
\affiliation{\institution{University of Illinois Chicago}\city{Chicago}\state{IL}\country{USA}}
\author{Ali Hoseinpour}
\affiliation{\institution{University of Illinois Chicago}\city{Chicago}\state{IL}\country{USA}}

\begin{abstract}
Issue-labeling services now apply LLM labels to open-source issues automatically. We evaluate one
as a deployed, selective service: Qwen2.5-7B, zero-shot and with retrieved examples, against
classical classifiers given the same input, on the NLBSE'24 benchmark and on issues the same five
repositories received in 2025--26, after the model's training cutoff. TF-IDF is the most accurate on
the benchmark (75.5\%) but falls to 66.3\% on 2025--26 issues and 60.4\% on a new repository; the LLM
is stable (70.9\%, 75.3\%, 68.0\%). A confidence threshold tuned for 10\% error among auto-labeled
issues overshoots in every condition (up to 13.3\% in distribution, 27.6\% under shift). With
thresholds fitted and certified on separate data, Learn-then-Test certifies 10\% error only for
TF-IDF (11\% of issues auto-labeled); the LLM is first certified at a 20\% target, and no certificate
carries over to a new repository. Leave-one-repository-out calibration restores conformal coverage
but not threshold risk. Controlled rewordings show that the phrase ``sorry if this is a silly
question'' pushes the few-shot LLM toward the label \emph{question} (+14.8 points vs.\ a neutral
sentence), while newcomer status alone has a small effect; deferral keeps such flips from being
auto-applied but halves the share of these reporters' issues labeled automatically. Evidence that
the LLM's quotes drive its decisions is inconclusive. Input novelty detects a new repository within
13 requests with under 1\% false alarms; the model's own confidence rarely notices. A record/replay
regression suite catches injected calibration and retrieval bugs but barely catches prompt and
preprocessing bugs.
\end{abstract}
